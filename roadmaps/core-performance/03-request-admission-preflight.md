## Component Baseline And Decision Gate

Measure actual request construction, capture, detached canonical/control/crypto
preparation, final entry, initial materialization/authentication and renewal as
separately observed subcomponents, with their complete aggregate total retained.
Use a genuine grant issuer, original local claim and current authenticated runtime
selection. Declare exact function/task/source/definition cardinalities, byte caps,
host lifetime/reprepare rules, pool settings, concurrency and cold/warm policy.
No fake activated handle, bare anchor/claim success or omitted renewal is allowed.

At activation, attribute owner reads, allocations, canonical/crypto work and
reprepare/invalidations; distinguish driver await from CPU and lock envelopes.
Choose one actual owner improvement and record its before/after proof and numeric
budget. Do not assume more transaction fusion, stable cross-host reuse or an
immutable certificate from repetition alone. Existing narrow issuer reuse is a
candidate only when all original scope, generation, installation and lifetime
correlations remain exact.

## Breaking Changes, Cleanup And Validation

Replace selected private composition in its real owner and migrate connected
callers. Delete displaced duplicate preparation/validation and compatibility
glue. Retain ordinary Application, standalone, rerun, redispatch and durable
readers for their real consumers. A new identity/format/schema/authority or
activation decision requires explicit disposition; do not silently reinterpret
outstanding sessions or broaden host reuse.

Focused PGlite and ordinary-role PostgreSQL must retain bounded scalar refusal
before payload, function/task/cold aggregate caps, unchanged-digest corruption,
foreign/clone/closed/wrong-context refusal, lazy error order, revocation/head/source
changes, post-wait deadlines, replay before invalid-source binding, source failure
rollback and all five absent child families. Preserve original locks and full
tail attestation. Test capacity concurrency and pre-handoff cancellation,
unknown forwarded COMMIT, cleanup/interruption and no pre-confirmation install.
Complete initial auth/renewal and same/different scope/request contention remain
real obligations; no SQL spans control/crypto/native callbacks.

Run affected type/source/lint checks, both reviews and substantive-fix reruns;
clean up and commit the coherent iteration. Component comparisons retain setup,
retries and all outliers. A declared group milestone or cross-component/lifecycle
contract change triggers full mixed performance integration measurement; local
owner improvements use component evidence plus bounded connected correctness.
