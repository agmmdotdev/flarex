# Protected Final Publication

Status: implemented within approved preflight 76. Final publication reuses the
initial complete evidence; settled replay and frozen acceptance remain separate
open gates.

The coherent outcome is one full verification of the locked installation at
finalization, followed by exact terminal, installation, readiness, availability,
event and head writes in the same target-owned transaction. Existing publication
owners continue to validate and issue their own evidence from actual stored
occupants. Their independent full readers remain available for recovery, audit
and binding. No new readiness certificate, schema, identity or settlement owner
is introduced.

Finalization authenticates a complete head and receipt prefix, checks its
independent inventory and observes the catalog. Its terminal and publication
repositories reuse those prerequisites through their common writers. Actual
returned identifiers must equal the restored occupants. Event and progress
records advance only the exact issued terminal, installation and readiness
sequence; they issue no restored-history authority. The phase witness requires
one receipt issuance and root payload per step during publication.

Retain the full initial head, exact claim/lease/fence and prefix checks, catalog
validation, original receipt producers, canonical publication codecs, conflict
occupant semantics, event sequencing, exact head CAS and atomic availability.
Native acquired-connection protection and the explicitly functional-only PGlite
factory remain mandatory boundaries. Existing immutable prerequisites may be
handed directly to their owning write/restoration operations within this protected
finalization transaction. Every newly affected row must still be selected or
returned and decoded, and all normalized references must agree with that evidence.

Factor each retained repository's common insert and actual-occupant restoration
so the protected finalizer and independent corroborating entry point converge on
one writer. Keep prerequisites with their existing issuers; plain event/progress
records must never masquerade as restored history. Extend the existing operational
head/event mechanics only for the exact terminal/publication transitions, with
unchanged completed position and tail after termination. The full finalizer must
not become a collection of reconstructed graphs after each append.

Reject a transaction-wide graph cache, a configurable fast mode, fabricated
successful write results, a second SQL implementation, and reusing mutable head
or availability decisions from an earlier transaction. Initial readiness still
requires the full catalog proof. Uncertain COMMIT continues through the existing
excluded recovery session and complete stored evidence; it does not trust the
unreturned in-memory publication result.

Validation retains the complete phase witness and adds actual-row/normalized
reference/conflict/CAS alterations during finalization. A rejected publication
must leave no terminal, publication, event or head advance, and a clean retry
must succeed. Run focused owner tests plus fresh/additive/restart and consumer
regressions on PGlite and ordinary-role PostgreSQL. Preserve cancellation,
rollback-only, catalog mismatch and COMMIT-success/response-loss behavior.
Require reduced repeated reconstruction, affected checks, both scoped reviews,
cleanup and a coherent commit. Frozen performance acceptance and wider inventory
proof remain separate overall gates; ShippingProfile activation stays paused.
