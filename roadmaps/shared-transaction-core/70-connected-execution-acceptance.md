# Connected Execution Acceptance

Status: selected core execution acceptance and retirement disposition complete.

## Outcome And Preflight

Close the remaining execution gates through the implemented native, CMS and
existing commerce/composite entry points. Use the existing finite comparison and
load workloads, inspect their exact obligations, correct connected defects at
their actual owners, and audit retirement. This is the completion package in
[the finite plan](./62-core-completion-plan.md), not another benchmark platform.

The current scalar references retain preparation without the shared runtime
introduced by [scoped hosts](./69-scoped-prepared-hosts.md). Migrate their private
inputs and returned operations to that owner before comparing requests. Preserve
independent request execution and publication SQL; sharing lifetime, admission
and recovery mechanics must remain explicit. Require a runtime and expose close,
including refusal and draining witnesses. Remove their unowned preparation path.
No production selector, reference fallback or additional settlement owner.

## Integrity Decision

Retain immediate accepting-transaction verification of the selected metadata,
including replay and recovery. Preparation remains immutable, bounded and
host-owned; it is never an admission verdict. Activation, catalog, ordered,
unique and relation lifecycle, publication, binding and installation owners keep
their current validation and fencing responsibilities.

Framework history protection restricts ordinary updates/deletes, but privileged
repair can disable it. Application publication/catalog/build writers do not
enforce one dependency-complete readiness-root invalidation protocol. An unchanged
root therefore cannot replace dependency verification in the selected contract.
Keep existing ordinary-role and privileged unchanged-digest tamper witnesses;
none moves to an asynchronous audit. Retain and report the selected-dependency
read/hash cost. This does not promise constant-size warm admission, nor does it
scan unrelated shops' complete histories.

Protected-root replacement is not selected for this goal. It would require a
new enforced writer/invalidation contract and an explicit changed privileged
corruption detection boundary, rather than a cached checksum. No schema, durable
format or data-deletion transition is authorized by this acceptance decision.

## Acceptance And Retained Limits

The selected coordinated protocol preserves distinct profiles. Native final
admission and CMS command execution overlap ordinary same-scope publication;
sequence-consuming materialization still serializes. Existing direct-SQL commerce
and composite callbacks enter publication protection before business DML. Their
admission overlaps, not their business writes. A future generic-commerce cutover
must preserve those semantics or replace them with a separately proven protocol.

The scalar references now share explicit prepared-host capacity, preparation
charging, use/close and drain ownership. Their independent execution/publication
remains test-only. They serialize commands, while core validates concurrent
observations; the comparison does not isolate a universally removable wrapper
cost. The final bounded normal comparison misses the declared relative-latency
target for thirty-two-row inserts. One-row reads meet the median-ratio threshold
but miss the required per-pair consistency. Other admitted profiles meet their
targets in this sample; variation between runs does not establish a causal
improvement. Several observer calibrations miss tolerance, so instrumented
latency is not accepted as neutral. Neither five pairs nor short load bursts
establish stable tails or many-shop capacity.

The hot-scope twelve-slot load exceeds the ten-connection pool and still misses
the zero-refusal target at final publication. Confirmed rollback and explicit
retry pass, but failed offered requests remain throughput failures. The four-scope
grid completes without refusals in the bounded sample; this does not isolate the
cause of every timeout or establish sustained capacity. Preserve the existing
deadlines and report offered/completed work separately from retries.

The connected Pricing regression exposed an incomplete lifetime handoff: its
workflow facade omitted the core host's close operation, and its shared-database
fixture retained obsolete workflow hosts across cases until capacity refused
further preparation. Forward the existing close operation without adding another
lifetime owner, release test-owned hosts after each case, and prove closed replay
refuses without changing durable state. Keep the shared preparation limit. This
is completion of an existing core consumer migration, not new Medusa capability.

Native source attribution includes local Workerd startup/bridge and shared
admission/finalization; it does not isolate a material avoidable repeated-write
journal cost. Conditional journal coalescing is therefore not selected on the
available evidence. Retain the current journal's latest receipts, raw logical
write events and independently authenticated seal: syscall replay, overlay,
canonical verification and stored-attempt recovery still consume them. This is
not a finding that journal overhead is small. A later workload-specific correction
must attribute the cost and migrate all those readers together.

## Completion Decision

The finite execution plan is complete for its selected contracts: coordinated
execution/admission and publication, bounded prepared-host ownership with fresh
integrity verification, and connected correctness/measurement/retirement
acceptance. Affected existing consumers have moved; the retained paths above
have current users and explicit replacement gates. No correctness failure or
unmigrated required execution consumer is being deferred as a performance limit.

The numerical misses above remain limitations. This completion does not claim
sustained capacity, all-framework logical storage/migration consolidation, new
Medusa integration, general schema evolution, developer APIs or hosted activation.
